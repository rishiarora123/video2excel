Built for recordings that scroll through a list (e.g. an attendees list). Each record's three lines become Name, Position, Organization. Overlapping/duplicate cards from the scroll are de-duplicated automatically. 2 fps captures everyone including people only briefly on screen at the start/end of the scroll; drop to 1 for the cleanest text if you don't need those edge rows.

Each sampled frame is embedded as an image with its frame number and timestamp.

Runs locally · ffmpeg for frames · openpyxl for the workbook
